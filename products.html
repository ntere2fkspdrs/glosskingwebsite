Fast Finish Ceramic Paint Prep

Benefits

  • A value-for-money spray-on product for a superior shine and finish after a general car wash.
  • The ceramic coating helps minimise the impact of corrosive road grime and acids for a long-lasting finish, and helps maintain that professional detail look.
  • Protect and shine with minimal effort, as often as you like.

Testing

  • The Gloss King team found it easy to apply, with a superior shine compared with other products on trial.
  • After 4 weeks and 4 car washes with standard car wash detergent, the vehicle still held its shine.
  • Strong water-beading (hydrophobic) effect that limits water adhesion. Wiping dry with a Gloss King Detailing Towel made drying only a few minutes.

Safety

  • Tested and checked to ensure it meets the chemical make-up described in the safety (MSDS) information on this website.
  • Following the safety information keeps you and your family safe.

Application

  • Start with the car completely washed and dried, out of direct sun and extreme heat.
  • Spray on the vehicle exterior and wipe evenly with a Gloss King microfibre towel.
  • Leave for 90 seconds, then polish to a shine with a Detailing Towel.
  • Older vehicles with faded paint may benefit from a polish first. Re-wash the vehicle after polishing.
