About us

Gloss King is an all-Australian family-owned business working and operating from South-East Queensland.

Our Gloss King team have a collective experience in the Australian automotive industry of more than 60 years in car detailing, vehicle preparation, service and support and dealership management.

As car enthusiasts ourselves, we understand the frustration of trying to find good value car care products to keep our vehicles, whether they're collectables or daily runners, looking their best. There is certainly a myriad of product options, and promises made by car care retailers that don't necessarily meet customers' expectations. How do you find products you can trust when most retailers are making the same claims?

Our mission

To offer well tested, safe and value for money aftermarket car care products, applicators and polishing equipment for the do-it-yourself car enthusiast, and anyone who really cares about keeping their car, SUV, truck or ute in top condition.

Australian car users now have the safe choice – Gloss King!

Hand selected and tested here

All products have been hand selected from car care manufacturers from around the globe and tested here in Australia. We use key car care product users (DIY and detailers) to give feedback on ease of application, quality and finish.

Customer reviews are an ideal way of informing new "to be" users, and we'd love to hear from you.
