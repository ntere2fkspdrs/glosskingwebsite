The Gloss King Story

Gloss King is an all-Australian family-owned business working and operating from South-East Queensland.

Our team has a collective experience in the Australian automotive industry of more than 60 years in car detailing, vehicle preparation, service and support, and dealership management.

As car enthusiasts ourselves, we understand the frustration of trying to find good-value car care products to keep our vehicles, whether they're collectables or daily runners, looking their best. There are a myriad of options, and plenty of promises from car care retailers that don't always meet expectations. How do you find products you can trust when most retailers are making the same claims?

Our mission

To offer well-tested, safe and good-value aftermarket car care products, applicators and polishing equipment for the do-it-yourself car enthusiast, and anyone who really cares about keeping their car, SUV, truck or ute in top condition.

Australian car users now have the safe choice – Gloss King!

Hand selected and tested here

Every product is hand-selected from car care manufacturers around the globe and tested here in Australia. We rely on keen DIY users and professional detailers to tell us how easy each product is to apply and what they think of the quality and finish.

Customer reviews are a great way to help new users, so we'd love to hear from you.

Safety first

Safety is our key priority. We've worked with manufacturers to make sure every product is safe, hazard free and a pleasure to use. Read more on our safety page.
